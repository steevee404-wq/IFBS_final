Why Choose Us
About our School
Founded in 2021, The International Franco British School (IFBS) provides a comprehensive, dual-curriculum education. Our program integrates both the British Cambridge system and the French national curriculum, offering students a broad academic foundation. This approach supports the development of bilingual skills and a global perspective, preparing our students to be confident, well-rounded individuals ready for success on the international stage.

Why Choose a British-Style Education at IFBS?
At IFBS we leverage the internationally renowned British curriculum to provide a rigorous academic foundation. Our school transforms these standards into a dynamic learning experience that builds independence, confidence, and real-world expertise.
However, what truly sets us apart from other international schools is the advantages that only our British system can offer:
✓
Cultivating Critical Inquiry
We move beyond rote memorization, teaching students to analyze, debate, and solve complex problems with confidence.
✓
Global Academic Recognition
The British system offers a seamless path to elite universities worldwide, and at IFBS, we ensure students are exceptionally prepared for that journey.
✓
A Focus on the Whole Student
Our environment prioritizes both academic achievement and personal growth, ensuring students develop essential leadership and social skills.
✓
Dynamic, Student-Centered Learning
We offer a structured yet flexible framework that allows students to master core concepts while discovering their individual passions and talents.

CELEBRATING DIVERSITY
Embracing Global Cultures

Here is a paragraph focused on celebrating diversity, keeping with the professional, inclusive, and student-centered tone of your website: At IFBS, we consider our diverse student body to be one of our greatest strengths and a vital part of the educational experience. We actively celebrate the rich variety of cultural backgrounds, languages, and unique perspectives that our families bring to the school. By fostering a deeply inclusive environment where every individual feels seen, respected, and valued, we ensure that our students are learning not just from their teachers, but from one another. This vibrant daily exchange of ideas and traditions perfectly complements our Cambridge curriculum, ultimately shaping our students into open-minded, empathetic, and truly global citizens.

MISSION

To build on the proven quality of the British curriculum while fostering an international perspective towards education. To champion academic excellence and create an inspiring place for children and young people to act and achieve the best version of themselves.

STATEMENT OF SCHOOL AIMS
Statement of School Aims

The International Franco-British School is a vibrant, co-educational school which balances its modern outlook with traditional British values. We aim to provide the highest standards of learning and teaching to ensure all our students fulfil their academic potential regardless of their ability.

The school also provides compassionate and dedicated pastoral support throughout all levels of the school via its highly effective pastoral system. This means each student feels valued, respected, and supported throughout their time at IFBS.
