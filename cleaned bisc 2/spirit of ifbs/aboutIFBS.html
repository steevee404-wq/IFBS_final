Why Choose Us
About our School
Founded in 2021, The International Franco-British School offers a premier education designed to cultivate curious, confident, and global-minded learners. Grounded in a rich European heritage and built upon the world-renowned British educational framework, our school provides a dynamic environment where academic rigor meets creative inquiry. We empower students with sharp critical thinking skills, intercultural empathy, and the adaptability needed to excel on the global stage. Through a warm, student-centered community, we inspire every individual to discover their passions, setting the foundation for lifelong success and leadership in an interconnected world.

Why choose a British style education?
Ultimately, the characteristics of British education are what truly determine the effectiveness of our school. Our commitment to education is based on teaching processes which have proven successful for generations in the UK, and by integrating them with our unique Moroccan twist we know this style of education best suits the needs of our student's.
However, what truly sets us apart from other international schools is the advantages that only our British system can offer:
✓
Quality pre-university education
Our system prepares students for their time at university.
✓
Globally accredited qualifications
iGCSE, A Level and BTEC qualifications are universally recognised.
✓
A proven academic environment
All our students have gone on to access tertiary education at different universities around the world.
✓
A holistic approach
Education is not simply confined to the classroom and so our co-curricular programme allows everyone to find their true niche in life.
Therefore, be part of this amazing journey and come to see why IFBS is the best choice for your child's educational journey.
OUR VALUES

Education at IFBS is a precious and profound gift. It has the power to influence the course of a child's life and ultimately, shapes who we all are and what we believe in.

Therefore, the foundations of our school are laid on the following four key values:

Respect Excellence Compassion Rigour
CELEBRATING DIVERSITY
Embracing Global Cultures

With over 57 differing nationalities from Europe, North & South America, Asia, Africa, and Oceana we are truly an international school. As you would expect, we joyfully celebrate the numerous special global events which happen throughout the year; from the delights of our Winter Fair to the solemnity observed during Ramadan, we encourage all our students to be respectful of each other's culture.

We prepare our community of students to be confident global leaders in tomorrow's world – constantly curious, kind and changing. Morocco has always been an outward looking country and so we want our students to be as curious and ambitious as their antecedents.

MISSION

To build on the proven quality of the British curriculum while fostering an international perspective towards education. To champion academic excellence and create an inspiring place for children and young people to act and achieve the best version of themselves.

‘Aspire, Act, Achieve’
STATEMENT OF SCHOOL AIMS
Statement of School Aims

The International Franco-British School is a vibrant, co-educational school which balances its modern outlook with traditional British values. We aim to provide the highest standards of learning and teaching to ensure all our students fulfil their academic potential regardless of their ability.

The school also provides compassionate and dedicated pastoral support throughout all levels of the school via its highly effective pastoral system. This means each student feels valued, respected, and supported throughout their time at IFBS.
