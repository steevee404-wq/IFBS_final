Pastoral Wellbeing

Education is the key to unlocking potential and shaping a purposeful future.

We believe that true academic success is rooted in a student’s personal growth and sense of security. Our approach treats pastoral care and academic progress as deeply interrelated, ensuring that every student receives the compassionate guidance necessary to thrive both inside and outside the classroom.

Empowering Pupils Beyond the Curriculum

Building Essential Life Skills for a Brighter Future

Studying these key areas outside the curriculum gives every pupil an equal opportunity to develop the skills and knowledge they need to thrive now and in the future. This includes helping them deal with the issues they face every day — friendships, emotional wellbeing, and change — and gives them a solid foundation for whatever lies ahead, so they can face an uncertain world with hope.

A Culture of Care

Cultivating a Safe and Inclusive School Community

At the International Franco-British School, pastoral care is woven into the fabric of daily life. We provide a nurturing environment where every child feels valued, heard, and supported. Through dedicated mentorship and open communication, we build strong relationships that empower students to take intellectual risks, celebrate diversity, and develop a profound sense of belonging.
