Our Aims

At IFBS, we blend a vibrant, co-educational environment with our cherished values. We are committed to defining the future of education by empowering students to reach their full potential. Our strategic direction is guided by a clear sense of purpose and a forward-thinking ambition that ensures every learner is prepared for the challenges of tomorrow.

★

Academic Excellence

We do this by providing a broad and engaging academic curriculum, which encourages intellectual curiosity as well as fostering a lifelong love of learning.

Rooted in strong core values, our approach ensures that students not only strive for academic excellence but also grow into compassionate, responsible, and ethical members of society.

●

Pastoral Support

Our aim is to provide the highest standards of learning and teaching to ensure all our students fulfil their academic potential, regardless of their ability.

We recognize that true academic success goes hand-in-hand with emotional wellbeing, which is why exceptional pastoral care is central to our approach, ensuring students feel happy, secure, and ready to thrive.

●

Health and Safety

Above all else, we aim to safeguard the health and safety of everyone at our school, as well as ensuring the emotional well-being of all our students in an empathetic and nurturing environment.

We uphold rigorous daily health standards, from maintaining impeccably clean facilities to enforcing strict hygiene and safety protocols across all classrooms and play areas.

'Aspire, Act, Achieve'

Our Vision

To be the premier choice for families seeking an academic environment where intellectual independence, ethical integrity, and global perspective converge to produce the leaders of the next generation.

Our Mission

To deliver a rigorous, dual-system education that balances academic discipline with personal growth, equipping students with the critical thinking skills necessary to navigate an increasingly complex world.
