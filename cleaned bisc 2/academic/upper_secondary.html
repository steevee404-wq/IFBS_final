Background

Cambridge Upper Secondary is a pivotal stage designed for learners aged 14 to 16 years. This phase builds upon the critical thinking and foundational knowledge developed in Lower Secondary, introducing students to the internationally recognized Cambridge IGCSE (International General Certificate of Secondary Education) curriculum. The focus here is on academic rigor, deep subject mastery, and preparing students for the crucial examinations that will open doors to higher education globally.

Options & Personalization

At this stage, students are given the autonomy to begin tailoring their educational journey. While we ensure a strong academic core remains in place, students select from a wide variety of optional subjects that align with their university aspirations and personal passions.

This personalized approach empowers students to take ownership of their learning, keeping them highly engaged as they explore the disciplines they wish to pursue at the Advanced level and beyond.

Compulsory elements

While flexibility is a hallmark of the IGCSE program, universities look for a well-rounded academic profile. To ensure our students meet these global standards, certain core disciplines remain mandatory:

  • Mathematics: Essential for advanced logical reasoning and analytical skills.
  • English Language: Crucial for effective communication and academic writing across all subjects.
  • The Sciences: Students must select at least one core science (Biology, Chemistry, or Physics) to maintain strong inquiry skills.

Guided Teaching Hours

The IGCSE curriculum demands a higher level of focus and specialized instruction. The academic timetable is strictly optimized to ensure each chosen subject receives the recommended 130 hours of guided learning. This time is expertly divided between intensive theoretical instruction, collaborative seminars, and hands-on practical application—such as laboratory work for the sciences. Our dedicated faculty also provides extensive revision support and exam technique preparation to ensure students are confident and ready.

Assessment opportunities

Assessment in Upper Secondary culminates in the formal Cambridge IGCSE examinations at the end of the two-year program. These are the world’s most popular international qualifications for 14 to 16-year-olds, widely recognized by leading universities and employers worldwide.

The assessments are rigorous and varied, designed to cater to different learning styles. Depending on the subject, students may be evaluated through a combination of written examinations, oral tests, practical laboratory assessments, and structured coursework. This comprehensive evaluation ensures a true reflection of a student's hard-earned capabilities.
