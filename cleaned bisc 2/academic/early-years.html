The EYFS

The Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) is the framework that guides how we care for and teach children from birth to age five. It sets the standards for learning, development and care that help every child get the best possible start.

At [School name], the EYFS shapes how our classrooms are set up, how our staff plan each day, and how we work alongside families to support every child's early years.

Image placeholder

Guiding Themes

01

A Unique Child

Every child is a competent learner from birth, and we celebrate each child's own pace, personality and potential.

02

Positive Relationships

Children learn to be strong and independent through warm, secure relationships with the adults and peers around them.

03

Enabling Environments

Our classrooms and outdoor spaces are designed to spark curiosity and support learning through play.

04

Learning & Development

Children develop and learn at different rates, and our teaching adapts to reflect the different ways in which children grow.

Areas of Learning

The EYFS framework is built around seven areas of learning, split into three prime areas and four specific areas.

Communication & Language
Physical Development
Personal, Social & Emotional Development
Literacy
Mathematics
Understanding the World
Expressive Arts & Design
