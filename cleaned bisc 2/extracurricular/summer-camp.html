Extracurricular

Summercamp

Our IFBS summer camp blends high-energy outdoor adventures, creative workshops, and interactive learning to keep children active and inspired all break long. It's the ultimate space for students to build lasting friendships, discover new hobbies, and make unforgettable memories in a fun, supportive environment.

01

Study & Academic Support

Beyond targeted exam preparation, we offer dedicated study sessions designed to help students strengthen foundational skills, catch up on reading, or simply explore new subjects at their own pace. Our quiet, supervised spaces provide the perfect environment for learners to build strong study habits and stay academically engaged throughout the summer.

02

IGCSE & A-Level Preparation

Keep academic skills sharp over the summer break. Our expert-led study sessions provide targeted, structured support for IGCSE and A-Level students, helping them master challenging concepts, build exam confidence, and get a head start on the upcoming year.

03

Creative Arts

Let imagination take the lead! Our engaging art workshops encourage self-expression through painting, drawing, and hands-on crafting, giving students a relaxing space to explore their creativity and discover new talents.

04

Aquatic Games

Cool off and dive into the fun! From friendly pool relays to high-energy water games, our aquatic activities are the perfect way for students to stay active, build teamwork, and make a splash on sunny summer days.

05

Culinary Workshops

Step into the kitchen and whip up something delicious. Our cooking sessions teach essential culinary skills, nutrition, and kitchen safety, all while letting students enjoy the tasty rewards of their own hard work.

06

Sports & Athletics

Keep moving and build team spirit on the field. We offer a dynamic range of sports focused on fitness, coordination, and friendly competition, ensuring every child finds a game they love while staying healthy and active.
