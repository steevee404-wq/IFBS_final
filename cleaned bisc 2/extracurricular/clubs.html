Extracurricular

Clubs

Beyond the classroom, pupils at IFBS are encouraged to explore new interests, build friendships and develop skills through a wide range of clubs held throughout the school week.

01

Robotics Club

Pupils design, build and programme their own robots, taking on hands-on challenges that bring maths, engineering and computer science to life.

02

Arts club

A relaxed, creative space where pupils experiment with painting, drawing, sculpture and mixed media. The club encourages self-expression and gives pupils' work a home in our termly exhibitions.

03

Football Club

Open to all ability levels, football club builds teamwork, fitness and sportsmanship through weekly training sessions and friendly matches against other schools.

From 3 to 18 years old
