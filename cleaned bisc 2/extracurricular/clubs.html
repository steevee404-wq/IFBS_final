Extracurricular

Clubs

Beyond the classroom, pupils at IFBS are encouraged to explore new interests, build friendships and develop skills through a wide range of clubs held throughout the school week.

01

Robotics Club

Pupils design, build and programme their own robots, taking on hands-on challenges that bring maths, engineering and computer science to life. The club meets weekly and culminates in an end-of-term showcase.

02

Debate & Public Speaking

Pupils learn to research, structure and present an argument with confidence. Through friendly debates and mock trials, they build the critical thinking and communication skills that carry well beyond the classroom.

03

Art & Design

A relaxed, creative space where pupils experiment with painting, drawing, sculpture and mixed media. The club encourages self-expression and gives pupils' work a home in our termly exhibitions.

04

Football Club

Open to all ability levels, football club builds teamwork, fitness and sportsmanship through weekly training sessions and friendly matches against other schools.

05

Music & Choir

Pupils rehearse together each week, building musicianship and confidence on stage. The choir performs at school assemblies, seasonal concerts and community events throughout the year.

06

Model United Nations

Pupils step into the shoes of world leaders, researching global issues and debating policy in a simulated UN assembly. MUN sharpens negotiation, research and diplomacy skills for our older pupils.

From 3 to 18 years old
