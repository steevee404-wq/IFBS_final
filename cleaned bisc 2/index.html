Welcome to IFBS

A Dual Curriculum, A Global Outlook

The International Franco-British School (IFBS) brings together the academic rigour of the British Cambridge system and the depth of the French national curriculum in a single, coherent programme. From our earliest years classes through to our senior students, every child is guided to become bilingual, confident, and genuinely prepared for life beyond school.

Our campus in La Marsa is a place where curiosity is encouraged, character is built alongside academic ability, and every student is known as an individual — not just a number on a register.
