Come and see IFBS for yourself

The best way to understand what makes IFBS special is to walk our corridors, meet our teachers and see our pupils at work. Our Open Days give prospective families a genuine feel for school life, with dedicated sessions for every age group, from Early Years right through to our Advanced Programme.

What to expect

Your visit, step by step

01

Welcome & registration

Arrive at the main entrance, where our admissions team will greet you and check you in for your session.

02

Institute tour

Explore our classrooms, labs, library and sports grounds, guided by staff and current pupils.

03

Meet the teachers

Sit in on a taster lesson and chat directly with the teachers who would be working with your child.

04

Admissions Q&A

Ask our admissions team about the application process, fees and next steps over refreshments.
